    What this is (and is not)

    Constraint programming is an established research technique for music composition (used in academic systems since the 1990s). ConstraintLab is a transparent browser implementation: the rules, the search, and the rejections are all visible on this page. It runs entirely on your device - no account, no server, no network calls.

    Current limits, stated plainly: root-position triads only, four voices, one 16-bar AABA form with the A phrase repeated identically, harmonic minor or major keys. The leading-tone rule applies to the soprano, within each phrase and across the B-to-A seam; parallel fifths/octaves and the upper-voice leap limit are also enforced across the phrase seams. This is a composition toy with a proof, not a replacement for a composer.
